# Classic (2.0.0 B's launcher, 6.20): its screens (tools/preview-shell.sh with PREVIEW_THEME=classic),
# one PNG each in build/shell-preview, to hold against the 2.0.0 B preview's (tools/launcher-preview at
# v2.0.0b). It starts on the Wii U side's Home.
wait 90
shot classic-home             # Continue playing, Recently played
press right                   # the second button: Graphic packs
wait 20
shot classic-home-second
press down                    # the recent games
wait 20
shot classic-home-recent
press up up                   # Launch game, then the header
wait 20
shot classic-header
press right                   # Library, in the header
press cross
wait 45
shot classic-library          # Your games: the list beside the game's details
press down down
wait 25
shot classic-library-down
press r1                      # Settings
wait 45
shot classic-settings         # Preferences beside On this console
press cross                   # the first page, as a dialog
wait 30
shot classic-settings-page
press circle
press r1                      # About
wait 45
shot classic-about
press l1 l1 l1                # back to Home
wait 40
press circle                  # Change emulator: the sides in the header
wait 20
shot classic-sides
press right cross             # the 3DS side
wait 90
shot classic-3ds-home
press cross                   # Launch game
wait 30
shot classic-launch
